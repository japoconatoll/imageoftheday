IMAGEOFTHEDAY

Every night at midnight a program goes through the front pages of about sixty Italian newspapers, finds the photographs printed on them and keeps one: the image repeated on the most front pages that day, or, when nothing repeats, the largest. It is cut out of the page as it was printed and posted with its date.

No one chooses.

A project by Jacopo Natoli, 2026.
Front pages from Il Post's daily review of the press. Photographs belong to their authors and publishers.
